GTA IV Map Exporter
===================

1. Double-click "GTA IV Map Exporter.exe".
2. Check the three paths at the top (GTA IV folder, Blender, where to save the .blend).
   GTA IV and Blender are found automatically on most machines.
3. Tick the districts you want (or "Select all" for the whole city).
4. Click "Export to Blender" and wait for the progress bar to finish.
5. Click "Open in Blender".

Needs Blender 4.2 or newer installed. Keep the .exe next to the "_internal" folder.
Textures are saved in a "<name>_textures" folder beside the .blend; move them together.
The whole map needs about 3.6 GB of free space and takes about 6 minutes.
